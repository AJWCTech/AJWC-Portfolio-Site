Why this archive exists

Most of this work is no longer available from the university. The GitHub repositories tied to the course were pulled, and the Minerva / Ultra links went dead — some when staff left, the rest when I graduated. None of it was archived for the students who produced it.

I passed the degree and I am still paying for it. So I rebuilt what I had kept locally into this permanent archive. For a lot of this coursework, this site is now the only place it exists.
