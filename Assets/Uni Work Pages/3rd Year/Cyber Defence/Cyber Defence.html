Cyber Defence & Risk Management

This report analyses threats to enterprise systems and proposes layered mitigation strategies based on a defence-in-depth model. The objective is to evaluate organisational risks, design proportional security controls, and recommend pragmatic solutions. Technical focus includes risk assessment, control frameworks, and industry-specific cyber defence strategies.

Executive Summary

The report evaluates potential cyber threats to enterprise environments and proposes mitigation strategies aligned with defence-in-depth principles. Analysis considers industry-specific risks and operational vulnerabilities, recommending layered controls to reduce exposure. Outcomes emphasise proportional risk management and actionable security improvements for organisational resilience.
