Cyber Resilience

This cyber resilience plan addresses the operational and security challenges created by the ransomware attack on Teletainment Ltd, which resulted in significant infrastructure loss and service disruption. The objective is to prioritise secure system reconstruction, risk mitigation, and long-term operational resilience through structured cyber security controls and recovery strategies. Technical focus centres on risk assessment, defence-in-depth principles, and resilient architecture design to ensure business continuity and protection against future threats.

Executive Summary

This cyber resilience plan addresses the significant operational disruption caused by a ransomware attack on Teletainment’s infrastructure. With core services compromised, the objective is to prioritise secure system reconstruction, risk mitigation, and operational continuity. The plan focuses on identifying critical vulnerabilities, implementing robust security frameworks, and establishing recovery strategies to ensure long-term organisational resilience.
