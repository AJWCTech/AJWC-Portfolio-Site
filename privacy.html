Cookies

This site sets one cookie, and only at the moment you submit the contact form. It is a standard PHP session cookie used to limit how many times the form can be submitted in a short period, which is what stops it being used to send spam.

It contains no personal information, it is not used to track you, and it is deleted when you close your browser. Because it is strictly necessary for a service you have asked for, it does not require consent — which is why you are not being shown a cookie banner.

There are no analytics cookies, advertising cookies or third-party trackers on this site. Fonts are served from this domain rather than from Google, so reading the site does not reveal your visit to anyone else.

Contact form messages are kept in my email account for as long as the conversation is useful, and in any case no longer than 24 months, after which they are deleted. If we go on to work together, correspondence may be kept longer where I need it for business records or tax purposes.

You can ask me to delete your message sooner at any time.
