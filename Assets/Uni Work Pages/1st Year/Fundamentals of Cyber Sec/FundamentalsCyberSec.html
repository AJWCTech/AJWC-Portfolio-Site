Fundamentals Of Cyber Security

This research report explores commercial cybersecurity threats within the case study organisation, analysing potential risks and their impact on operations. It demonstrates academic and professional understanding of cybersecurity terminology and contested concepts while proposing practical mitigation strategies. The objective is to evaluate threats and outline actionable steps to enhance organisational security and resilience.

Executive Summary

This research examines the growing cybersecurity threats faced by UK organisations, with a focus on protecting customer data and operational systems from cybercrime. It analyses potential risks, identifies common attack methods, and evaluates frameworks and controls that can mitigate these threats. The report also considers the role of cybercriminal actors, the consequences of inadequate security, and strategies for recovery. The objective is to provide practical recommendations that enhance organisational resilience and data security.
